## 2. Which readings of the paper are needed

Three readings were needed to reproduce Table 15. Each is a switch, not a tuned factor:

- **Tail-boom load (`tail_boom_load_mode`).** `paper` evaluates Eq. 55 with K_ht taken
  literally from Eq. 5, K_ht = (l_ht/c)·A, so the boom shear is q|Cm|c³/l_ht: Eq. 54 divided by
  the aspect ratio. `eq54` is the self-consistent moment balance, Q = q|Cm|bc²/l_ht and M = Q·l_tb.
- **Spar sizing radius (`sizing_radius_mode`).** `paper` uses K_tube = r/c̄ (Eq. 15, with c the mean
  chord as §2.2.2 defines it) times the sizing chord c_root (§3.3), i.e. an effective radius
  r·c_root/c̄. `physical` uses r. The two are identical for a rectangular wing.
- **UBI11's general factors.** Table 15's UBI11 column was computed with UBI11's *individual*
  f_ht, f_vt, f_cb and f_gear from Table 14. The combined f_wing and f_tb are used as for the
  other aircraft (see `configs/verification/ubi11.yaml`).
