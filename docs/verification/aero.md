Notes:
- The Trefftz-plane drag from OAS's own circulations matches its near-field drag, so the VLM
  force integration is self-consistent.
- VLM and lifting line differ by a few % in e and C_L,alpha at AR 6. Lifting line is the
  cruder model for a low-AR rectangular wing; the VLM has finite chord. The two errors partly
  cancel in total C_D.
- The strip C_Dp is higher than the 2D value because the tip strips fly at lower c_l, where
  NACA 4412's drag is above its minimum.
- The VLM is a flat-plate model: camber does not enter it, so its alpha is measured from zero
  lift. Strips are matched on c_l, and NeuralFoil supplies the true section alpha (`alpha_sec`).
