### AirCrafter 1.0: grid refinement


The full grid at 2x the points per dimension (four gridded dimensions: mass, span, AR, speed) is 16x the points, about 6.3 h single-threaded; at 4x it is 256x, about 4.2 days. A full 2x run was started and stopped after 1 h (too slow for what it answers). Instead the grid is refined locally, on the same grid points as the full grid (the box keeps the full grid's points inside it): **top20** = the extents of AirCrafter 1.0's 20 best design points plus one default step, every speed; **dp** = the selected point +/- 2 default steps in mass, span and AR, speeds covering every top-20 point's M2 and M3 speed. A local refinement cannot find a better optimum outside its box. The box option (`ac1_run.m` opts.box) was added after the main timing commit and is committed with this page; it only selects grid points, the per-point model is unchanged. The cost per point inside the boxes is higher than the whole grid's average: the per-point cost varies across the grid (not investigated). Default grid (whole): best score 4.3850 (M2 + M3).

## 5. Cost of a complete design study

AirCrafter 1.0: one sweep covers every motor/prop/mass/span/AR/speed combination of its grid: 24 min single-threaded.

AirCrafter 2.0 multipoint: 35 runs in outputs/results/runs (the 9 prop pairs at 6S, 4S/5S on the best pair, 10 multistarts, bookends, the normalisation grid, the bottle sensitivity), 1588 driver iterations (objective evaluations) in total; at the clean single-thread 4.0 s per evaluation (gradients included, averaged) that is 1.8 h (estimate: clean cost per evaluation x recorded evaluations). The recorded wall times of those runs (3 in parallel, 3 threads each) sum to 1.9 h. The minimum a team needs is the enumeration plus multistart on the best case: 1.1 h.


RDO (d = 7): 51 s per driver iteration clean; the 3 starts took 139 driver iterations, about 2.0 h serial single-threaded (estimate), on one propulsion case.
