**Reading it.** The means agree within the Monte Carlo standard error for every output and every rule. For the constraints UDR's σ is within 0.5 % of the d = 7 Monte Carlo: they are close to additive in the parameters. For the 3-lap time UDR's σ is -9.5 % off while Smolyak level 2 is +1.2 %, so the lap time has interaction (cross) terms between the screened parameters that the univariate decomposition cannot represent (the powertrain parameters act together: Kv, Rm and the thrust factor all set the thrust and power curves). The screening adds 2-7 % (the 8 parameters held at their means), so against the full Monte Carlo UDR is low on σ by about 15 % for the lap time and 2-6 % for the constraints. In the robust objective μ + kσ (k = 1) that is about 0.1 s of a 80 s lap; in the constraints, β σ is about 5 % short. Smolyak level 2 removes the propagation error at 8.5 times the nodes; it is one config line (`robust.quadrature: smolyak`) if that matters.

P(violation) at the deterministic optimum, Monte Carlo over all 15: energy_margin 52.2 %, rpm_margin 0.4 %, SM 49.7 %; every other constraint 0 %. The two active constraints fail about half the time, as an optimum on its constraints must.
