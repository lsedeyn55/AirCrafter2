# Mass-model calibration on three USYD aircraft

Generated by `studies/mass_calibration_usyd.py report` from `fit` (git aff3998); no hand-typed numbers. Plan, data checks and decisions: `docs/MASS_CALIBRATION_PLAN.md`. Model: `aircrafter2/mass/` (`docs/modules/mass.md`); fitting hook: `aircrafter2/mass/calibration.py` (G&G Eqs. 127-128, relative residuals, equal weights).

**Three aircraft is a small sample.** The leave-one-out (LOO) errors in §5 are the evidence for the scatter; the in-sample fits (§4) are not validation. The fuselage factors (f_cb, f_tb) and f_gear rest on two aircraft each ([OH] and [W], one tricycle and one taildragger; [W]'s fuselage is one lumped row), so they are weak.

Sources: [OH] Orange Hornet, USYD DBF 2023-24; [W] Project Woombah, 2024-25; [HB] Project Humpback, 2025-26 (printed page numbers); [GG] Garcia & Gamboa (2022).

- [HB] is used for the wing and tails only: Table 36's 6.14 lb 'Fuselage' also holds the gear, servos, receivers, receiver batteries, prop and wiring. Its mass basis is unclear (§8).

## 8. Report inconsistencies

- [HB] mass basis: CAD values 'validated against the measured weights … with a ≈20 % increase from CAD to manufacturing' (p. 43); whether Table 36 includes the 20 % is not stated (OPEN_QUESTIONS Q47).
- [HB] Table 36: the 'Mission 1' row reads 1.22 lb (the battery alone); M2 is 18.54 lb against 18.24 lb from its own rows; empty 9.93 + battery 1.22 = 11.15 lb against an M1 take-off weight of 8.6 lb (Table 33, p. 35) and an MTOW of 14.3 lb (pp. 3, 36).
- [HB] spar sizes: p. 26 text 0.71/0.63 and 0.55/0.47 in; Table 24 0.63/0.55 and 0.39/0.31 in; p. 37 5/8 and 3/8 in (all 0.04 in walls).
- [HB] airfoil 'SD3016' (p. 36) against S3016 (Table 24, p. 26; p. 37): the UIUC S3016 is used (Q51).
- [HB] wing area 868 in² against 59 × 14.57 = 859.6 in².
- [W] Table 34 has no servo row (Q48).
- [W] cargo-box width 4.7 in (Fig. 11, p. 19) against 4.72 in maximum width (Table 30, p. 31).
- [OH] midsection longerons: four 12/10 mm (p. 19) against four 10/8 mm (p. 39).
- [OH] M2 take-off weight 8.6 kg (Table 43) against 8.9 kg 'actual' (Table 44, p. 59).
- [OH] tube strength '3.13 MPa' (p. 55) against 313 MPa (p. 35): a typo.
- [OH] the wing has 1 mm plywood endplates (p. 21), not modelled (`winglet_ratio` 0; k_wing absorbs them).
- [OH] y_motor is not dimensioned in the drawings (scaled: 0.335 m, PLACEHOLDER).
- [OH] site: p. 25 uses 400 m (Wichita); the case uses 400 m.
