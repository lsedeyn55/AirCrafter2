# 2025-26 validation: Project Humpback as built

Generated by `studies/validation_2026.py report` (git `9d3e02d`) from `outputs/results_2026/validation_*.json` and `reference/aircrafter1/runs/ac1_2026_r1.json`; every number below is read from `docs/studies/results_2026/validation_2026.json`. Config `configs/humpback_2026.yaml` (extends `dbf2026_multipoint.yaml`; the ruleset `configs/rulesets/dbf_2025_26.yaml`). [HB] = 2026 USYD DBF Design Report (Project Humpback), printed page.

**Stated up front.** (i) **Out-of-sample:** the 'USYD without Humpback' column uses the mass factors fitted on Orange Hornet and Woombah only (`configs/mass_usyd_loo_humpback.yaml`), so Humpback's wing and tails are predicted, not fitted. Humpback's fuselage and gear were never in any fit ([HB] Table 36 lumps them, Q47): they are an extrapolation in every column. (ii) **Nothing is tuned to the flight data.** The flight cases set the throttle from the logged average battery current and the take-off mass from the logged take-off weight (ballast through the mass model's hook): both are input conditions of the flights, as in validation_2025.md. (iii) Calm air; take-off in the mission time; course `dbf_2025_360_mid` (the 2026 rules' figure, p. 17). (iv) Modelling differences: rectangular wing with end-plates (end-plates not modelled), flaps not modelled, the payload fuselage as a box sized by the payload counts (PLACEHOLDER law, Q55).

## 7. [HB] inconsistencies (listed, not resolved; Q57)

- Table 1 (p. 4) vs Table 43 (p. 59): M2 laps 9 vs 8; M3 laps 5 vs 4; M3 speed 49.9 vs 73.3 ft/s.
- Table 33 (p. 35, 42 passengers / 14 cargo, 9 laps) vs Table 43 'design' (30 / 10, 275 in banner).
- M2 take-off weight: Table 1 17.5 lb, Table 42 17.9 lb, Table 33 14.3 lb, §4.8.4 13.2 lb, Table 36 18.54 lb.
- Battery: 3380 mAh (§4.8.3 p. 30; 100.05 Wh at 29.6 V, over the 100 Wh rule), 3300 mAh / 97.68 Wh (§5.3.10 p. 42, §5.3.12), 3350 mAh (drawing p. 47). 3300 mAh is used.
- Wing: 59 × 14.57 = 859.6 in² against the printed 868 in² (p. 36); Table 24 c̄ 1.21 ft.
- Fuselage: passenger bay 39 in (p. 36; '3 ft 3 in', p. 22) longer than Fig. 9's 2 ft 9.4 in payload box; Fig. 9's 5 ft 1 in against p. 36's 70.5 in.
- Cost equation (Eq. 6, p. 7) without the rules' EF.
- Banner drag: car-tow C_D,b 0.019–0.028 (Table 41) against the flat-plate 1.2 (§4.8.5) and Table 29's ΔC_D 0.3025 on the wing area (Q56); Table 41 says all tests used 118 in banners but lists six lengths.
- Spar sizes: p. 26 text, Table 24 and p. 37 disagree; Table 36's 'Mission 1' row (Q47).
