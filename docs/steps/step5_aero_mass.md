# Step 5: coupled aero + mass (MDF)

Generated by `examples/aero_mass.py` from `configs/step5_aero_mass.yaml` (placeholder aircraft:
rectangular wing, NACA 4412 seed, V = 20 m/s, n = 5, m_pay = 1.5 kg, m_sys = 1.2 kg).

MDA: Newton (solve_subsystems) + DirectSolver over {cruise aero point, lift balance alpha:
L = W, G&G mass group with its own NLBGS closure}. Couplings: W -> lift balance; C_m,wing and
C_L,alpha -> loads (Eqs. 46, 47, 54); t/c, t at spar, A/c^2, P/c -> mass; tube radius from the
section thickness (D1).

- Strength-governed spar (gauge off): tube mass falls as t/c grows (t_sigma ~ 1/r^2 with
  r ~ t/c), the expected half of the trade. The G&G LE/TE stringer and rib masses scale with the
  section area, though, so the total wing mass still rises above t/c ~ 0.08.
- Gauge-governed spar (gauge on): the wall is fixed, so tube mass grows with r, i.e. with t/c.
- At this scale the drag change dominates the weight change (dD/dW ~ 2 D_i / W), so on this
  coarse grid D falls monotonically toward thin sections. What stops the thinning is the spar:
  section 3 shows the optimiser running into the tube-sizing limits.
